# English word frequencies

`english-freq.json` holds the 30,000 most common English words and how often each one appears, exported from [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer (the `en` list).

The wordfreq data is licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and draws on several sources credited in the wordfreq README, including Wikipedia, OpenSubtitles, Google Books Ngrams and the Leeds Internet Corpus. This file is redistributed under that same licence. The rest of inkprint is under the licence in `LICENSE.md`.

inkprint uses it as the baseline for the word list: a word that English uses often and you almost never do is out of character for you.
